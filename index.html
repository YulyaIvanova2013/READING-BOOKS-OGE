Read the Text

Read the Text

Reading is one of the best habits.

Reading helps people discover new things, widen their outlook and just relax.

There are two large groups of books: fiction and non-fiction.

Fiction books are most popular with readers and include short stories, different novels and poetry.

Non-fiction books include dictionaries, reference books, biographies and encyclopedias.

Today TV and the Internet have almost replaced paper books.

Modern technologies made it possible for us to use electronic readers.

On the one hand, such e-readers are very convenient, as they do not take up much space and weigh little.

They can be read in low light and even darkness.

The production of e-books does not need paper, so they are more environmentally friendly.

On the other hand, e-books have some disadvantages.

To begin with, they are rather expensive and not everyone can afford them.

Secondly, free electronic texts to download are hard to find.

Besides, paper books are more pleasant to hold in your hands and turn the pages.

In conclusion, it is impossible to live without reading, and even if paper books disappear in the future, people will never stop reading.
